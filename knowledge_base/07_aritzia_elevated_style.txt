==================================================
THE ARITZIA AESTHETIC
==================================================

Aritzia is a Canadian women's fashion brand founded in 1984 in Vancouver, BC. It operates through several sub-brands (Wilfred, TNA, Sunday Best, Contour, Denim Forum), each with a distinct sub-aesthetic that fits within an overall elevated, minimalist, and quality-focused ethos.

THE CORE ARITZIA PHILOSOPHY:
- Elevated basics: pieces that are more interesting than fast fashion but accessible enough for everyday wear
- Quality fabrics: Aritzia is known for its fabric selection — buttery knits, structured blazers, and clean-cut tailoring
- Understated polish: nothing overly trendy or loud; pieces are designed to feel current but not fleeting
- Versatility: the brand's pieces are designed to transition from casual to polished with minor styling adjustments

THE 2025 ARITZIA SIGNATURE LOOK:
"Minimalistic, elevated, and chic" — the dominant aesthetic entering 2025. Clean lines, neutral tones, one confident statement layer, and quality fabrics.

==================================================
KEY ARITZIA PIECES & HOW TO STYLE THEM
==================================================

THE EFFORTLESS PANT:
One of Aritzia's most popular items. Wide-leg silhouette, elevated fabric, drapes cleanly. Pair with a fitted turtleneck or cropped blazer and loafers.

THE SUPER PUFF JACKET:
Aritzia's signature outerwear. Available in multiple lengths. The minimalist formula: Super Puff + straight-leg jeans + clean white sneakers.

WILFRED TROUSERS & BLAZERS:
Wilfred (Aritzia's sub-brand) focuses on romantic, effortlessly sophisticated pieces. Wilfred trousers in a wide-leg silhouette paired with a simple fitted top = the brand's most recognizable workwear formula.

THE BABATON BLAZER:
Babaton (another Aritzia sub-brand) focuses on polished, office-ready tailoring. A Babaton blazer over a simple tank or bodysuit with tailored trousers covers everything from office to evening.

CONTOUR COLLECTION:
Form-fitting, body-conscious pieces in smooth, buttery fabrics. Pairs well with relaxed, draped pieces for contrast.

==================================================
ARITZIA OUTFIT FORMULAS
==================================================

WORKWEAR (The Aritzia Office Formula):
Tailored blazer (structured, clean lines) + straight-leg or wide-leg trousers + pointed-toe loafers or kitten heels + simple fitted top (bodysuit, ribbed tank, or silk blouse)
Key: Neutral tones — camel, ivory, charcoal, navy, black. One texture contrast. No logos.

ELEVATED CASUAL:
Oversized blazer (worn open) + simple white tee or ribbed tank + dark straight-leg jeans + clean white leather sneakers or loafers
Key: The blazer does all the work. Everything else is minimal.

WEEKEND CHIC:
Linen or modal trousers (wide leg) + cropped knit top + mule or strappy sandal + minimal jewellery
Key: Fabric quality reads as elevated even in casual contexts.

EVENING OUT (Aritzia-Style):
Silky slip dress or Effortless Pant in a rich color + simple strappy heeled sandal + understated gold jewellery + small clutch
Key: Restraint. One beautiful piece, nothing competing with it.

COZY & ELEVATED (Fall/Winter):
Oversized cashmere cardigan or knit coat + slim turtleneck + straight-leg dark jeans + ankle boot
Key: The oversized knit/coat is the statement. Keep the rest monochromatic or in analogous tones.

THE ARITZIA COLOR PALETTE
==================================================

CORE NEUTRALS ALWAYS IN ROTATION:
- Black (the foundational Aritzia shade)
- Ivory/Cream
- Camel / Tan / Butter
- Navy
- Charcoal Grey
- Soft White

SEASONAL ACCENT COLORS:
- Fall/Winter: Burgundy, Forest Green, Chocolate Brown, Rust
- Spring/Summer: Sage Green, Powder Blue, Blush Pink, Warm White

THE ARITZIA APPROACH TO COLOR:
Monochromatic or tonal dressing is the brand's signature. Rather than bold color-blocking or prints, Aritzia aesthetics favor different shades of the same family (all camel tones, all white-to-cream, all black-to-charcoal) or a single neutral base with one muted accent.

==================================================
ELEVATED MINIMALISM: THE PRINCIPLES
==================================================

1. FIT IS THE MESSAGE: In minimalist dressing, there are no prints or embellishments to distract. The fit of the garment IS the outfit. Invest in tailoring.

2. FABRIC IS EVERYTHING: A simple silhouette in a quality fabric (cashmere, silk, ponte, quality denim) looks expensive. The same silhouette in a cheap synthetic does not.

3. RESTRAINT IS SOPHISTICATION: One focal point per outfit. If the trousers are interesting (wide leg, pleated), the top should be simple. If the top is beautiful (silk, draped), the bottom should be understated.

4. MONOCHROMATIC = EFFORTLESS POLISH: Head-to-toe tonal dressing (all creams, all blacks, all navies) requires almost no thought and always looks intentional.

5. SHOES MAKE THE OUTFIT: In minimalist dressing, the shoe is often the single piece with the most visual impact. Invest in quality footwear.

6. QUALITY ACCESSORIES OVER QUANTITY: One great bag, one great pair of earrings, one great watch. Not many low-quality accessories trying to fill the space.

STYLING FOR A WARMER CLIMATE (Occasion-Specific for Aritzia)
==================================================

ARITZIA AT THE OFFICE (Four Seasons):
Spring: Wilfred blouse + Effortless Pant + white loafers
Summer: Linen blazer + silk tank + tailored shorts or wide-leg trouser + sandal
Fall: Babaton blazer + ribbed turtleneck + tailored trouser + pointed-toe ankle boot
Winter: Long wool coat (Aritzia outerwear) + cashmere turtleneck + wide-leg trousers + heeled boot

==================================================
ACCESSORY PHILOSOPHY IN ELEVATED MINIMALISM
==================================================

THE RULE: Accessories should complement, not compete. In a minimal outfit, one elevated accessory is all that's needed.

BAGS: Structured, medium-to-small, quality leather. The Aritzia aesthetic favors clean shapes over logos.
SHOES: Quality over quantity. A great loafer, a clean white sneaker, a well-heeled ankle boot.
JEWELLERY: Gold or silver, clean and minimal. Small hoops, a thin chain necklace, a simple ring stack.
BELTS: A quality leather belt in black or tan. Used to define the waist on blazers or trousers, not as a statement piece.
SUNGLASSES: Minimal frames. Cat-eye, oval, or classic wayfarer in tortoise or black.
