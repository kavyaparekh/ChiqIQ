DRESSING FOR YOUR BODY TYPE: THE COMPLETE GUIDE
Source: bestoutfitstoday.com, fashionuptoday.com, macys.com

==================================================
UNDERSTANDING BODY SHAPES vs. BODY TYPES
==================================================

BODY SHAPES describe your proportions (pear, apple, hourglass, rectangle, inverted triangle, diamond).
BODY TYPES describe your frame (petite, tall, slim/athletic, curvy, plus-size).

The goal is never to "fix" your body — it's to dress with intention, in clothes that work with your natural proportions to create the silhouette YOU want.

The universal principle: Balance proportions. If you're wider at the top, add volume at the bottom. If you're wider at the bottom, add interest at the top. If you're straight up and down, create curves.

==================================================
PEAR SHAPE
(Narrow shoulders, defined waist, fuller hips and thighs)
==================================================

GOAL: Balance wider hips by drawing attention upward and adding volume to the upper body.

BEST CHOICES:
- Off-shoulder, boat neck, square neck, or embellished necklines — add width to the shoulder
- Puff sleeves and ruffled tops — add visual volume to the upper body
- A-line skirts (flares from the waist) — skims over hips gracefully
- Fit-and-flare dresses — cinch at the waist, flare over hips
- Wide-leg pants — creates balanced proportions leg-to-hip
- Dark-wash bootcut jeans — elongate the leg and balance the hip line
- Wrap dresses — define the waist while accommodating hips

COLOR STRATEGY:
- Brighter colors, prints, and embellishments on top
- Darker colors on bottoms (dark jeans, black or navy trousers)
- This pulls the eye upward naturally

AVOID:
- Skinny jeans paired with fitted tops (overemphasizes hip-to-shoulder imbalance)
- Anything that adds bulk at the hip (patch pockets, heavy fabric below the waist)
- Pencil skirts that cling across the hips without flare

==================================================
APPLE SHAPE
(Fuller midsection, narrower hips and legs, less defined waist)
==================================================

GOAL: Elongate the torso, define the waist, and draw attention to the legs.

BEST CHOICES:
- Empire waist dresses — flow from just below the bust, skimming the midsection
- Wrap tops and dresses — create a defined waist through the diagonal closure
- Straight-leg and wide-leg pants — elongate the lower half
- V-necklines — elongate the neck and draw the eye vertically downward
- Monochromatic outfits in darker shades — create a long, lean visual line
- Vertical stripes — lengthen the frame
- Asymmetrical hemlines — draw the eye away from the midsection

COLOR STRATEGY:
- Darker colors on the torso; lighter or brighter shades on the legs
- Monochrome in dark neutrals for maximum elongation

AVOID:
- Tight waistbands that highlight the midsection
- Cropped tops unless layered over high-waist bottoms
- Heavy horizontal stripes across the torso
- Empire dresses that don't have enough flare below the bust

==================================================
HOURGLASS SHAPE
(Balanced bust and hips, well-defined natural waist)
==================================================

GOAL: Celebrate and emphasize the natural waist-to-hip ratio.

BEST CHOICES:
- Wrap dresses — the ultimate hourglass silhouette
- Belted styles — a belt at the natural waist is the single most powerful hourglass tool
- High-waist trousers and skirts — showcase the waist
- Bodycon dresses — designed for this shape
- Fit-and-flare dresses — naturally mirrors hourglass proportions
- Cropped tops with high-waist bottoms — shows the waist
- Tailored blazers — fit this shape beautifully when taken in at the waist

COLOR STRATEGY:
- Contrasting pieces (light top, dark bottom or vice versa) with a belt highlights the waist
- Color-blocking naturally draws attention to the waist transition

AVOID:
- Oversized, shapeless, boxy clothing — erases the waist that is the shape's main asset
- Very stiff, structured fabrics that don't follow the body's curves

==================================================
RECTANGLE / STRAIGHT SHAPE
(Similar width at shoulders, waist, and hips; minimal curves)
==================================================

GOAL: Create the illusion of curves, definition, and dimension.

BEST CHOICES:
- Peplum tops — add flare at the hip, creating a waist-to-hip ratio
- Ruffles and embellishments at the hip and chest — add volume and dimension
- High-waist pants with a tucked-in top — creates a waist where there isn't one
- Fit-and-flare and skater skirts — adds curve below
- Belted outfits — the most effective rectangle-shape tool
- Layering and textured fabrics — adds visual depth and dimension
- Horizontal stripes — strategically add width to bust or hip

COLOR STRATEGY:
- Color-blocking: different colors on top vs. bottom creates a visual break that suggests curves
- Bright or printed waistband draws the eye to the middle of the body

AVOID:
- Boxy or oversized cuts from shoulder to hip (reinforces straight silhouette)
- Very minimal, unstructured basics worn together without layering

==================================================
INVERTED TRIANGLE SHAPE
(Broad shoulders, narrower hips and waist)
==================================================

GOAL: Balance broad shoulders by adding volume and interest below the waist.

BEST CHOICES:
- Wide-leg pants and flared trousers — add visual width at the hem
- A-line skirts — adds volume and curve below
- Bold shoes and statement belts — draw the eye downward
- V-neck and scoop necklines — minimize the shoulder's visual width
- Simple, unembellished tops in darker colors
- Full/midi skirts with volume

COLOR STRATEGY:
- Dark, simple, unembellished tops
- Lighter colors and prints on the bottom half to draw attention downward

AVOID:
- Puffy sleeves, halter necklines, or off-shoulder styles — all add visual shoulder width
- Boat necklines — the widest horizontal neckline, not ideal here
- Embellishments and patterns on top

==================================================
DIAMOND SHAPE
(Narrow shoulders, fuller midsection, narrower hips and legs)
==================================================

GOAL: Add width to shoulders, minimize the midsection, and add interest at the legs.

BEST CHOICES:
- Off-shoulder tops and boat necklines — add shoulder width
- Structured shoulder details — padded blazers and tailored jackets
- Darker colors on the torso
- Flared pants and A-line skirts — add width at the legs for balance
- Wrap and empire-waist dresses

==================================================
BODY TYPES: FRAME-BASED STYLING
==================================================

PETITE (under 5'4"):
- High-waist pants elongate the leg — the single most important petite rule
- Cropped jackets keep proportions from overwhelming a small frame
- Avoid oversized or maxi pieces that swamp the silhouette
- Monochromatic dressing elongates the frame
- Nude/skin-tone shoes extend the leg visually

TALL:
- Maxi dresses and long skirts work beautifully on tall frames
- Oversized blazers and coats are proportionally balanced
- Horizontal stripes and color-blocking add visual breaks that work in tall proportions
- Bold prints are more impactful on taller frames

PLUS-SIZE / CURVY:
- Structured fabrics that hold their shape flatter more than drapey, clingy materials
- Wrap dresses are universally flattering
- Well-fitted, well-tailored pieces over "hiding" in oversized clothes
- Empire waist and A-line silhouettes are reliable go-tos

SLIM / ATHLETIC:
- Textured fabrics (ribbed knit, boucle, quilted) add dimension
- Layering creates depth and visual interest
- Belted styles create curves
- Ruffles, pleats, and volume in skirts and sleeves add femininity

==================================================
UNIVERSAL PRINCIPLES ACROSS ALL BODY TYPES
==================================================

1. FIT IS EVERYTHING: A well-fitted H&M blazer beats an ill-fitted designer piece every time. Tailoring transforms clothes.

2. BALANCE PROPORTIONS: Loose top + fitted bottom, or fitted top + loose bottom. Avoid all-loose or all-tight combinations.

3. USE COLOR STRATEGICALLY: Darker shades recede (minimize), lighter shades advance (draw attention). Use this deliberately.

4. INVEST IN PROPER UNDERGARMENTS: The right foundation garments transform how every piece of clothing sits on your body.

5. TRY MULTIPLE MIRROR ANGLES: Check back, side, and front. How you look walking away matters as much as straight-on.
