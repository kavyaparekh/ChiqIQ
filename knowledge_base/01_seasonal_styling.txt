SEASONAL FASHION GUIDE: WHAT TO WEAR AND WHEN
Source: fashonation.com, jdinstitute.edu.in

==================================================
SPRING STYLING
==================================================

Spring calls for adaptable layering and celebrates renewal through color. Key principles:

COLORS & PATTERNS:
- Pastels (blush, lilac, sage, sky blue), floral prints, light neutrals, soft whites
- Bright accent colors work well on accessories or statement pieces

FABRICS:
- Lightweight cotton, linen, chambray, rayon
- Light knits and jersey for cooler mornings

KEY PIECES:
- Denim jacket or light trench coat for layering over dresses and blouses
- Midi skirts or straight-leg jeans with loafers or fashion sneakers
- Loose blouses and flowy tops
- Lightweight scarves for cool mornings that can be removed later

STYLING TIPS:
- Layer strategically — mornings are cooler, afternoons warm up
- Balance floral tops with solid-colored bottoms in complementary neutrals
- Ballet flats, sneakers, and sandals all work for spring depending on occasion

==================================================
SUMMER STYLING
==================================================

Summer prioritizes breathability and bold self-expression.

COLORS & PATTERNS:
- Bold, vibrant tones, tropical prints, stripes, bright solids
- White, coral, turquoise, yellow, neon accents

FABRICS:
- Cotton, linen, chambray, moisture-wicking blends
- Avoid synthetics — polyester traps heat
- Loose-weave fabrics allow air circulation

KEY PIECES:
- Flowing sundresses and maxi dresses
- High-waist shorts paired with cropped or tucked-in tops
- Linen trousers and loose wide-leg pants
- Strappy sandals, espadrilles, platform slides

ACCESSORIES FOR SUMMER:
- Wide-brimmed hats for sun protection that also elevate any casual look
- Oversized sunglasses
- Lightweight tote bags and woven bags

STYLING TIPS:
- Silhouette over skin: a loose, breezy maxi can be more impactful and comfortable than shorts
- Bold prints work in summer when you couldn't get away with them otherwise
- Keep jewelry minimal — the heat makes layering jewellery uncomfortable

==================================================
AUTUMN / FALL STYLING
==================================================

Fall fashion is about warm layers, texture, and deep earth tones.

COLORS & PALETTES:
- Burgundy, mustard, olive green, burnt orange, rust, chocolate brown, forest green
- Deep jewel tones: emerald, ruby, sapphire for evening occasions
- Navy and camel for sophisticated neutrals

FABRICS:
- Chunky knits, corduroy, suede, denim, light wool
- Flannel and plaid for casual layered looks

KEY PIECES:
- Ankle boots or knee-high boots — the cornerstone of fall dressing
- Chunky sweaters and oversized cardigans
- Long cardigans worn open over simple dresses
- Leather or suede jacket over lighter tops
- Turtleneck underneath a blazer — the professional fall formula
- Straight-leg or wide-leg jeans in dark washes
- Plaid or wool scarf

FALL LAYERING FORMULAS:
1. Camel trench coat + turtleneck sweater + straight-leg jeans + ankle boots
2. Chunky knit cardigan (open) + simple midi dress + tall boots
3. Leather jacket + light top + wide-leg trousers + loafers
4. Blazer + turtleneck + tailored trousers + pointed-toe flats

==================================================
WINTER STYLING
==================================================

Winter requires warmth without sacrificing elegance.

COLORS:
- Black, charcoal grey, navy, ivory, cream
- Rich accents: burgundy, forest green, cobalt blue
- Pops of color via accessories: a bright beanie, red scarf, or colored gloves

FABRICS:
- Wool and cashmere for coats and sweaters — warm without bulk
- Fleece for casual cold-weather layering
- Faux fur as a texture accent
- Thermal base layers under everything

KEY PIECES:
- Investment coat: wool overcoat or tailored puffer — the most visible piece in your winter wardrobe
- Turtleneck sweaters and thick knit pullovers
- Wide-leg trousers with heeled boots for a polished cold-weather look
- Knee-high or combat boots
- Thermal leggings under everything
- Wool-lined accessories: gloves, beanies, oversized scarf

WINTER STYLING TIPS:
- Wool coat + chunky knit scarf + wide-leg trousers + heeled boots = polished winter formula
- Keep base layers slim-fitting so outer layers drape cleanly
- Monochromatic winter outfits in charcoal or navy look intentional and chic
- Colorful beanie or gloves add personality without looking overdone

==================================================
YEAR-ROUND UNIVERSAL PRINCIPLES
==================================================

1. INVESTMENT PIECES: Classic trench coat, quality denim in a dark wash, neutral knits, a black blazer — these work year-round with seasonal styling adjustments.

2. FABRIC APPROPRIATENESS: Lightweight cottons and linens for warm months; wools, cashmere, and heavier knits for cold months. Getting the fabric right matters more than the silhouette.

3. LAYERING AS STRATEGY: One well-chosen layer can transition an outfit across temperatures and occasions (e.g., a blazer over a summer dress extends it into fall).

4. ACCESSORIES SHIFT SEASONS: The same dress worn with sandals and a straw hat reads as summer; worn with ankle boots, opaque tights, and a cashmere cardigan reads as fall/winter.

5. TRANSITIONAL DRESSING: Light-to-mid weight fabrics like ponte, jersey, and thin denim work in both spring and fall. Build your wardrobe around these transitional fabrics.
