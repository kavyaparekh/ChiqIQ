HOLIDAY & FESTIVE OCCASION STYLE GUIDE
Source: dresscodebyjulia.com

==================================================
THE FIVE HOLIDAY STYLE CATEGORIES
==================================================

1. GLAMOROUS & FESTIVE
Best for: Formal holiday parties, New Year's Eve, black-tie galas

SIGNATURE PIECES:
- Sequined dresses or skirts — the quintessential holiday look
- Metallic fabrics: gold, silver, bronze
- Velvet in jewel tones: emerald green, ruby red, sapphire blue, amethyst purple
- Glittering accessories and embellished clutches
- Statement jewellery: chandelier earrings, stacked cuffs, layered necklaces

STYLING TIPS:
- When wearing a fully sequined dress, keep accessories and makeup bold but focused (one statement element at a time)
- Velvet midi dress + heeled mules + drop earrings = a complete, effortless festive look
- Metallic shoes or bag can transform an otherwise simple outfit into a holiday one

2. COZY & CHIC
Best for: Holiday brunches, casual family gatherings, holiday markets

SIGNATURE PIECES:
- Oversized cashmere or merino sweater paired with leather leggings or a midi skirt
- Festive patterns: fair isle knits, plaid, nordic prints
- Layering essentials: cozy scarves, tall boots, stylish wool coat
- Matching sweater sets in holiday-adjacent colors

STYLING TIPS:
- The balance of comfort and style is key — an oversized sweater over faux-leather leggings looks intentional
- Plaid blazer over a simple turtleneck + straight-leg jeans = effortlessly polished
- Let the texture do the work: a heavily knitted sweater needs minimal additional styling

3. CLASSIC ELEGANCE
Best for: Work holiday parties, dinners with extended family, concerts and theater

SIGNATURE PIECES:
- The little black dress is reliable, but consider also: espresso brown, deep burgundy, or midnight navy — not everyone looks their best in black
- Tailored blazer + dress trousers in winter white or deep red — a modern, non-dress option
- Silk and satin midi dresses in understated colors
- Pearl or crystal jewellery for a timeless finish
- Classic pointed-toe heels or kitten heels

STYLING TIPS:
- Classic elegance is about understatement: one beautiful fabric, one statement accessory, impeccable fit
- A silk slip dress with a cashmere cardigan thrown over it reads as effortlessly elegant
- Quality fabrics transform otherwise simple shapes — satin, silk, crepe

4. CASUAL FESTIVE
Best for: Friend gatherings, casual holiday parties, office dress-down Fridays in December

SIGNATURE PIECES:
- Holiday colors (red, green, gold, silver, white) worked into everyday outfits
- Graphic sweaters in tasteful holiday motifs (not kitschy)
- Dark denim + shimmery or metallic top + statement shoes = casual festive done right
- Holiday-print scarves or accessories to add festivity to a neutral base outfit

STYLING TIPS:
- One festive element is enough — a glittery top with simple dark jeans and boots reads as festive without trying too hard
- Holiday green doesn't have to mean Christmas-tree green: emerald, sage, and forest green are all appropriately festive
- Red accessories (red bag, red shoes, red belt) instantly signal the holiday season

5. ACCESSORIES & FINISHING DETAILS

FOOTWEAR:
- Velvet loafers: comfortable, festive, elevated
- Glittery or metallic pumps: transform any simple dress
- Knee-high boots: winter-appropriate and sophisticated
- Embellished sandals: for warm-weather holiday destinations
- Avoid: casual materials (canvas sneakers, flip-flops) at any formal or semi-formal holiday event

JEWELLERY:
- Gold or silver depending on your skin tone's undertone (gold for warm, silver for cool)
- Statement earrings when wearing an updo or low neckline
- Layered necklaces with a simple neckline
- Gemstone or crystal pieces for evening — the holiday season is the moment for them

BAGS:
- Metallic or beaded minaudière clutch for formal events
- Velvet pouch in a jewel tone for semi-formal
- Simple leather or suede in a neutral for casual festive gatherings

MAKEUP:
- Red lips: the timeless holiday classic — pairs with minimal eye makeup
- Glittery or smokey eye: pairs with a nude or berry lip, not both at once
- Berry/wine lips: deep and romantic for winter evenings
- One feature: either bold eye OR bold lip, never both maximally done

==================================================
CHRISTMAS PARTY SPECIFIC: WHAT TO WEAR
==================================================

CORPORATE CHRISTMAS PARTY:
- Festive but office-appropriate: sequined top + tailored trousers, or a velvet midi dress
- Avoid: overly revealing cuts (it's still a work context), all-denim, overly casual looks
- Appropriate hemlines: at or below the knee for conservative workplaces; mid-thigh maximum for creative industries

FRIENDS/FAMILY CHRISTMAS GATHERING:
- More latitude for fun: cocktail dress, festive co-ords, printed midi dress
- Christmas-green velvet dress = perennial favorite
- Rich reds, cranberry, and burgundy are flattering in December light (candles, dim lighting)

UGLY SWEATER PARTY:
- Lean in: the uglier the better. Pair with simple dark jeans and clean boots so the sweater remains the star.

==================================================
NEW YEAR'S EVE
==================================================

THE OCCASION FOR MAXIMUM SPARKLE:
- Fully sequined dress or skirt — this is the one night where more is more
- Gold or silver metallic dress
- Embellished jumpsuit for a modern take
- Black cocktail dress + metallic accessories + glitter eye = simple but impactful

TIPS FOR NYE:
- You will likely be in this outfit from 8pm to 2am+ — prioritize comfort in footwear
- A faux-fur stole or satin blazer over your dress handles the cold without ruining the look
- Midnight-to-morning comfort: bring a clutch big enough for a pair of fold-flat flats

==================================================
THE HOLIDAY STYLING PHILOSOPHY
==================================================

The holiday season is the one time per year when "too much" is rarely a problem. Sequins at noon, velvet in the morning, metallics at brunch — the season gives permission for all of it. The only real mistake is wearing something that makes you feel uncomfortable or unlike yourself.

The key: feel beautiful, feel comfortable, and feel like yourself — elevated.
