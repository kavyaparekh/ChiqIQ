THE COMPLETE GUIDE TO OCCASION DRESSING
Source: fashion-a-holic.com, groomingtalks.com

==================================================
THE DRESS CODE HIERARCHY (Most to Least Formal)
==================================================

Understanding dress codes is foundational to occasion dressing:

1. WHITE TIE — Floor-length gowns (women), tuxedos with tails (men). Extremely rare.
2. BLACK TIE — Formal evening wear. Floor-length dresses, elegant cocktail gowns, tuxedos.
3. BLACK TIE OPTIONAL — Similar to black tie but slightly relaxed. Long dress or sophisticated cocktail dress.
4. FORMAL/EVENING — Cocktail dress or elegant separates; suits or sport coat for men.
5. COCKTAIL — Semi-formal. Knee-length or midi dresses, dressy separates, suits without ties.
6. SMART CASUAL — Dark jeans with a blazer, chic tops, clean sneakers acceptable.
7. BUSINESS CASUAL — Blazers, dress pants, button-downs; no jeans or sneakers.
8. CASUAL — Comfortable everyday wear; jeans, t-shirts, flats, sneakers.

GOLDEN RULE: It is almost always better to be slightly overdressed than underdressed.

==================================================
WEDDINGS
==================================================

WHAT TO WEAR:
- Cocktail dress (knee to midi length) for daytime/indoor weddings
- Maxi dress or formal gown for evening/black tie weddings
- Sundress for outdoor casual garden weddings
- Elegant separates: silk blouse + tailored trousers

WHAT NOT TO WEAR:
- NEVER white, cream, ivory, champagne, or off-white — it reads as competing with the bride regardless of intent
- Avoid all-black for traditional religious ceremonies
- Avoid anything too casual (jeans, shorts, sneakers) unless explicitly stated in the invitation

STYLING TIPS:
- Match venue and time of day: outdoor daytime is less formal; indoor evening is more formal
- Invest in one versatile wedding-guest outfit and vary accessories for different weddings
- Florals, pastels, and jewel tones are always safe bets
- Comfortable shoes matter — you will be on your feet for hours

==================================================
JOB INTERVIEWS
==================================================

THE UNIVERSAL INTERVIEW OUTFIT:
Blazer + simple top (blouse or fitted turtleneck) + dress trousers + clean leather or pointed-toe flats/heels

This formula works for 90% of interviews across industries.

INDUSTRY-SPECIFIC ADJUSTMENTS:
- Finance/Law: Full suit or blazer + trousers in navy, charcoal, or black. Conservative and polished.
- Tech/Startups: Smart casual — blazer optional, neat dark jeans acceptable, no athletic wear.
- Creative Industries: Express personality while remaining polished — interesting accessories, one statement piece.
- Retail/Hospitality: Clean, well-fitted, and brand-aligned. Research the brand's aesthetic beforehand.

KEY PRINCIPLES:
- Dress one level above the company's everyday dress code
- Nothing distracting: no strong fragrance, minimal jingling jewellery, neat grooming
- Fit matters more than price — a well-tailored mid-range blazer beats an ill-fitting designer one

==================================================
DATE NIGHT / SOCIAL OCCASIONS
==================================================

CASUAL DATE:
- Well-fitted jeans + a nice top (silk, cashmere, or interesting fabric) + ankle boots or block-heel mules
- A midi dress with sneakers reads as effortlessly cool for daytime dates
- Avoid anything that looks like you tried too hard for a casual setting

DINNER DATE (Nice Restaurant):
- Midi dress or jumpsuit in a rich fabric
- Tailored trousers + silk blouse + heeled sandals
- The LBD (little black dress) is a reliable choice — elevate it with statement earrings and a heel

COCKTAIL PARTY / EVENING EVENT:
- Wrap dress, bodycon, or structured cocktail dress
- Velvet or satin in jewel tones for evening
- Embellished top + wide-leg trousers as a modern alternative to a dress

BRUNCH / CASUAL GATHERING:
- Sundress or flowy midi dress
- Linen trousers + tucked-in top
- Matching co-ords set in a neutral or print

==================================================
PROFESSIONAL WORK SETTINGS
==================================================

BUSINESS PROFESSIONAL:
- Tailored suit (blazer + matching trousers or skirt)
- Sheath dress with a blazer
- Classic pumps or pointed flats in neutral colors
- Structured tote or leather bag

BUSINESS CASUAL:
- Blazer over dress shirt or blouse
- Dress pants, chinos, or tailored skirts
- Loafers, block heels, clean leather shoes
- No sneakers, flip-flops, or overly casual items

SMART CASUAL OFFICE:
- Dark jeans (no distressing) + blazer + nice top
- Ankle trousers + cashmere sweater
- Clean sneakers in neutral colors are acceptable

==================================================
FESTIVE / HOLIDAY PARTIES
==================================================

CORPORATE HOLIDAY PARTY:
- Festive but professional: sequined top + tailored trousers, or a velvet midi dress
- Avoid anything too revealing — it's still a work context

FRIEND/FAMILY HOLIDAY GATHERING:
- More latitude: cocktail dress, festive co-ords, holiday-themed prints
- Rich colors (emerald, ruby, sapphire) or metallics

NEW YEAR'S EVE:
- Sequins, metallics, glitter — this is the occasion for maximum sparkle
- Embellished midi dress or shimmer skirt + bodysuit

==================================================
BUILDING AN OCCASION CAPSULE WARDROBE
==================================================

The recommended minimum capsule for occasion dressing:

1. ONE dark suit or tailored set — covers interviews, formal events, and professional settings
2. ONE cocktail-appropriate outfit — a versatile midi or cocktail dress for weddings and parties
3. ONE smart-casual standby — dark jeans + a blazer + a great shoe
4. 2-3 accessories that transform these core pieces (statement earrings, a great belt, a silk scarf)

BUDGET-FRIENDLY TIPS:
- Rental services (Rent the Runway) for one-off formal occasions
- Thrift and consignment for occasion wear — formal clothes are often barely worn
- Invest in fit: take mid-range pieces to a tailor for a custom feel
